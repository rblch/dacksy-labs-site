2. The short version

We only process personal data where it is needed to run this website or to answer your messages. This website sets no cookies, stores nothing on your device, uses no analytics or tracking tools, and loads no content from third parties such as fonts, maps, or videos.

3. Hosting and server logs

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When you visit the website, your browser automatically sends information that GitHub stores in server log files:

  • IP address
  • date and time of access
  • page or file requested
  • amount of data transferred and status code
  • browser type, browser version, and operating system
  • referrer URL (the page you came from)

This data is technically necessary to deliver the website to you and to keep it secure and stable, for example to detect and fend off attacks. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; our legitimate interest is running the website securely and reliably. GitHub decides how long it keeps these log files; we have no access to them.

This may involve transferring data to the USA. GitHub is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, so the transfer is covered by an adequacy decision of the European Commission (Art. 45 GDPR). You can find more in GitHub’s privacy statement.

4. Contacting us by email or phone

When you contact us by email or phone, we process the details you give us, such as your name, email address or phone number, and the content of your message, in order to handle your request.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR where your request relates to a contract or steps before entering into one, and Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR otherwise; our legitimate interest is answering inquiries. We delete your data once your request has been fully dealt with, unless statutory retention obligations apply (for example under § 257 of the German Commercial Code or § 147 of the German Fiscal Code).

Our email runs on Gmail, a service of Google (for users in the EU: Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland). This may involve transferring data to the USA; Google is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (Art. 45 GDPR).

5. Links to other websites

This website links to other websites, for example dackelapp.com. No data is sent to the operator of a linked site until you follow the link. The privacy notices of that site apply there; our apps have their own privacy notices.

6. Encryption

This website is only delivered over an encrypted connection (TLS). You can tell by the “https://” in your browser’s address bar.

7. Your rights

You have the right to access the data we hold about you (Art. 15 GDPR), and to rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), and data portability (Art. 20). Just write to us at dacksylabs@gmail.com.

Right to object: Where we process your data on the basis of Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, you can object to the processing at any time on grounds relating to your particular situation (Art. 21 GDPR).

You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR). The authority responsible for us is the Landesbeauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit Nordrhein-Westfalen, Kavalleriestraße 2–4, 40213 Düsseldorf, Germany, www.ldi.nrw.de.

8. Further information

You are not obliged to provide us with personal data. Without the connection data listed in section 3, however, the website cannot be displayed. We do not use automated decision-making, including profiling.

We update this privacy policy when the website or the legal requirements change.
